PocketPlay

Support

PocketPlay is an Apple Watch app with three casual games: Mini Golf, Super Pong, and Stacker. Everything runs locally on your watch — no account, no internet connection, and no data ever leaves your device.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Open PocketPlay on your Apple Watch and swipe between pages to pick a game. Each game has a Play button on its home page.

How does the Mini Golf level editor work?

Tap "New Course" from the Mini Golf page. Use the Digital Crown and on-screen tools to shape, stretch, and bend the fairway, then place obstacles from the palette.

My scores or progress disappeared.

Progress is stored locally on your watch. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, or restored your watch, local data is cleared as part of that process — this is normal watchOS behavior for local app storage.
