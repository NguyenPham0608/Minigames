Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Data collection

PocketPlay does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics. The app has no network access, no accounts, no sign-in, and no third-party SDKs.

Data storage

Game progress — scores, custom golf courses, and settings — is stored entirely on your Apple Watch using standard local app storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Third parties

PocketPlay does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

Children's privacy

PocketPlay does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.
